03 Store POS App

A checkout that knows every customer before the order reaches the store counter.

A shared-device point of sale for store counter staff, built for the busiest hour, accountable by design.

  • Scan or search. Rotating and static printed codes both work; name search covers the code that won't scan.
  • Balance check before charge: name, role, program, and balance on screen; low-balance warnings, and hard blocks when funds run out.
  • Quick amounts or keypad (5 / 10 / 15 / 20), receipt after every charge, then straight back to scanning.
  • Runs in your currency. Concourser supports over 20 out of the box, so balances, charges, and reports all read in the one your campus uses.
  • Live daily totals with per-program breakdown, and a printable end-of-day PDF report. Every charge records the staff member who made it.

04 Admin Dashboard

Mission control for the whole campus.

Security & compliance

Built like ground control:
nothing moves unverified.

Concourser handles identity, attendance, and financial activity for hundreds of people a day, so the security concern is not an afterthought.

Encrypted in transit

All traffic between every app, dashboard, and the server is encrypted end to end in transit. Logging out clears the device session.

Short-lived rotating codes

Meal and attendance QR codes rotate and expire in seconds; attendance codes are single-use. A screenshot is worthless a moment later, and lost printed cards are revoked centrally.

Per-app isolated storage

Each app keeps its session in isolated local storage; one surface can never read another's session. POS customer details are shown only at the moment of a charge, never retained on the device.

Your data stays on your campus

Concourser supports lightweight, institution-controlled and self-hosted deployment as well as lower-cost managed hosting. Each institution can select the configuration that best fits its infrastructure, budget, data-residency and protection requirements.

In the field

In quiet, daily service.

Concourser runs the everyday rhythm of a working campus: morning check-ins, the busiest store hours, the end-of-day report. It is not built to impress with dashboards; it is built to stay out of the way.

It also pays for itself. Before Concourser, the piloting campus ran on hand-signed paper coupons: unverifiable at a busy store counter, four times the staff work, and money quietly leaking with every service. Short-lived rotating QRs and per-program cost-center reconciliation closed that gap, recovering an estimated several thousand dollars as savings every month since February 2026.

Planned & upcoming

In the blueprint,
not yet in orbit.

These are the directions we are building with founding campuses, not a shipped feature list. Each one extends something already running in daily service, and pilot partners help decide the order.

// Planned

Sessions beyond the classroom

Attendance sessions already run every morning. The same system building block extends to guest lectures, seminars, orientation and exam halls: one-off attendance lists, walk-in registration, and catering drawn against the event's own cost center rather than a student's program allowance.

// Planned

One app, every platform

The unified iOS beta on TestFlight carries all three roles in a single build. The next step is one cross-platform client for iOS and Android, so a campus deploys one app instead of any other number of installable surfaces.

// Planned

Financial and deadline calendars for everyone

Every entry in the ledger already carries a timestamp. Surfaced as a shared calendar, that becomes something each role can plan against: when an allowance refreshes or expires, when attendance is required, when a store settles, when a term closes and the books have to balance.

// Planned

Expanding to sponsors, guardians and collaborators

The role model already decides what each surface is allowed to see. Opening it wider brings in the people around a campus: sponsors funding a program, guardians following a student's balance, and partner institutions running a joint course or event, each with a wisely limited view and aggregate figures where privacy requires them.
